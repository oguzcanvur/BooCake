# Credits and licenses

Every third-party asset used by BooCake is listed here. Only the files the game actually uses are kept in the repo
(`src/assets/`); each folder also contains the pack's original `License.txt`.

## Effects and sounds: Kenney (CC0)
All packs are by [Kenney](https://kenney.nl) and released under **Creative Commons Zero (CC0 1.0)**: free for any use,
no attribution required (we credit anyway).

| Used for | Pack | Source | Files in repo |
|----------|------|--------|---------------|
| Particles (sparkles, smoke, flame, glow) | Particle Pack 1.0 (downscaled from 512 px to 128 px) | https://kenney.nl/assets/particle-pack | `src/assets/fx/` |
| UI and game sounds | Interface Sounds 1.0 | https://kenney.nl/assets/interface-sounds | `src/assets/audio/` |
| Bell and coin sounds | Impact Sounds 1.0 | https://kenney.nl/assets/impact-sounds | `src/assets/audio/` |

## Font: Fredoka (SIL Open Font License 1.1)
Fredoka by The Fredoka Project Authors, installed from npm as `@fontsource/fredoka`.
License: https://openfontlicense.org (the license text is in `node_modules/@fontsource/fredoka/LICENSE`).

## Shop background: made by the team with AI
`src/assets/bg/shop.webp` (the shop interior behind the counter: walls, window, shelves, display case) was
generated by the team with an AI image tool. It is not taken from any existing game.

## Workbench stations: made by the team with AI
`src/assets/stations/*.png` (dough bowls, ovens, plate, piping bags, topping jars, tray, trash can) were cut out
of one sheet the team generated with an AI image tool; the white background was made transparent.

## Customer characters: made by the team with AI
`src/assets/chars/*.png` (werewolf, vampire, ghost, witch, mummy, zombie) were cut out of one sheet the team
generated with an AI image tool; the white background was made transparent.

## Logo: made by the team with AI

`src/assets/ui/logo.webp` (BooCake logo: cupcake with bat wings and ghosts) was generated by the team with an AI
image tool; its dark purple background was made transparent. The favicon in `index.html` is cut from the same picture.

## Everything else
All other art except the particle textures is vector art drawn in code, so it needs no license: the counter,
workbench, all customer monsters
(pastel cartoon style chosen by the team), cupcakes,
buttons, icons and panels (`src/objects/`, `src/ui/`).

## How to add a new asset
1. Prefer CC0 or another license that allows free use in a school project.
2. Copy **only the files you use** into `src/assets/<type>/` together with the license text.
3. Add a row to the table above (what it is used for, pack name, source URL).
